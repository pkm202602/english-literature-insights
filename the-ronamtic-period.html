1798 – 1832 A.D.

The Romantic Period

A passionate literary revolution prioritizing emotion, individual imagination, sublime nature, and rebellion against industrial logic.

Core Literary Movements & Innovations

Primacy of Emotion & Imagination

Replacing Neo-Classical restraint with spontaneous feeling, creative intuition, and deep emotional sensitivity.

Sublime & Nature Worship

Viewing nature as a living divine force, a moral guide, and a source of aesthetic awe and spiritual restoration.

Individualism & Byronic Hero

Focus on solitary identity, non-conformity, passion, and the tragic, melancholic genius defying societal norms.

Supernatural & Gothic Romance

Fascination with medieval folklore, atmospheric terror, psychological shadows, and mysterious occurrences.

Movements & Generations

1798 – 1815 A.D.

First-Generation Romantics

Pioneered by the Lake Poets. Defined by nature worship, rustic simplicity, pantheism, and conversational poetic diction.

Key Figures: William Wordsworth, Samuel Taylor Coleridge, and William Blake (precursor).
1810 – 1824 A.D.

Second-Generation Romantics

Marked by intense passion, political radicalism, tragic youth, sensual beauty, and romantic idealization of classical Greece.

Key Figures: Lord Byron, Percy Bysshe Shelley, and John Keats.
1798 – 1832 A.D.

The Romantic Novel & Fiction

Emergence of Gothic horror, Regency novels of manners and social observation, and historical romantic fiction.

Key Figures: Jane Austen, Mary Shelley (Frankenstein), and Sir Walter Scott.

Master Poets, Novelists & Essayists

William Wordsworth 1770 – 1850 A.D.

Lyrical Ballads, The Prelude, Tintern Abbey

Samuel Taylor Coleridge 1772 – 1834 A.D.

The Rime of the Ancient Mariner, Kubla Khan, Biographia Literaria

John Keats 1795 – 1821 A.D.

Ode to a Nightingale, Ode on a Grecian Urn, Endymion

Percy Bysshe Shelley 1792 – 1822 A.D.

Ozymandias, Ode to the West Wind, Prometheus Unbound

Lord Byron 1788 – 1824 A.D.

Childe Harold's Pilgrimage, Don Juan, She Walks in Beauty

William Blake 1757 – 1827 A.D.

Songs of Innocence and of Experience, The Marriage of Heaven and Hell

Jane Austen 1775 – 1817 A.D.

Pride and Prejudice, Sense and Sensibility, Emma

Mary Shelley 1797 – 1851 A.D.

Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus
