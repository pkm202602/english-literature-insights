The Georgian Period (1910 – 1936)

Overview

The Georgian period usually references the reign of King George V. In a literary and cultural context, it overlaps significantly with the early Modernist phase, marked by the immense trauma and societal transformation brought on by the First World War.

Georgian Poetry

This era is highly celebrated for the "Georgian Poets," a group whose works were published in a series of anthologies by Edward Marsh. Their poetry initially focused on pastoral themes, nature, and romanticism before shifting heavily into the gritty, realistic themes of war poetry.
