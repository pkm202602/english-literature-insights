## 🏛️ Chronological Overview of Literary Eras


This project organizes English literature into **8 major chronological periods** and their respective sub-divisions:


### 1. ⚔️ Old (Ancient) English Period (450 – 1066 A.D.)
* **Main Hub Page:** [`the-old-english-period.html`](the-old-english-period.html)
* **Sub-Period:**
  * [`the-anglo-saxon-period.html`](the-anglo-saxon-period.html) (450 – 1066 A.D.) — *Anglo-Saxon invasion, heroic oral poetry, scop culture, and Beowulf manuscript.*


### 2. 🏰 Middle English Period (1066 – 1500 A.D.)
* **Main Hub Page:** [`the-middle-english-period.html`](the-middle-english-period.html)
* **Sub-Periods:**
  * [`the-anglo-norman-period.html`](the-anglo-norman-period.html) (1066 – 1340 A.D.) — *Norman conquest, chivalric romances, and trilingual literary culture.*
  * [`the-age-of-chaucer.html`](the-age-of-chaucer.html) (1340 – 1400 A.D.) — *Geoffrey Chaucer, The Canterbury Tales, and social realism.*
  * [`the-dark-or-barren-period.html`](the-dark-or-barren-period.html) (1400 – 1485/1500 A.D.) — *War of the Roses, printing press introduction by Caxton, and Arthurian prose.*


### 3. 🎨 The Renaissance / Early Modern Period (1500 – 1660 A.D.)
* **Main Hub Page:** [`the-renaissance-period.html`](the-renaissance-period.html)
* **Sub-Periods & Movements:**
  * [`preparation-for-renaissance.html`](preparation-for-renaissance.html) (1500 – 1558 A.D.) — *Rise of Humanism, Thomas More, and Italian sonnet introduction.*
  * [`the-elizabethan-period.html`](the-elizabethan-period.html) (1558 – 1603 A.D.) — *Golden Age of drama, University Wits, Marlowe, and Spenser.*
  * [`the-jacobean-period.html`](the-jacobean-period.html) (1603 – 1625 A.D.) — *Darker theatrical realism, Metaphysical poetry, and King James Bible.*
    * [`age-of-shakespeare.html`](age-of-shakespeare.html) (1590 – 1616 A.D.) — *The Four Great Tragedies, comedies, and late romances.*
  * [`the-caroline-period.html`](the-caroline-period.html) (1625 – 1649 A.D.) — *Cavalier poets, Carpe Diem themes, and civil conflict.*
  * [`the-commonwealth-period.html`](the-commonwealth-period.html) (1649 – 1660 A.D.) — *Puritan Interregnum, Cromwell, Milton's political prose, and Hobbes.*
  * [`puritan-period.html`](puritan-period.html) (1620 – 1660 A.D.) — *Religiously charged prose, Milton, Bunyan, and closure of public theatres.*


### 4. 🕯️ Neo-Classical Period (1660 – 1798 A.D.)
* **Main Hub Page:** [`the-neo-classical-period.html`](the-neo-classical-period.html)
* **Sub-Periods:**
  * [`the-restoration-period.html`](the-restoration-period.html) (1660 – 1700 A.D.) — *Age of John Dryden, Comedy of Manners, and reopening of theatres.*
  * [`the-augustan-period.html`](the-augustan-period.html) (1700 – 1745 A.D.) — *Age of Alexander Pope, Jonathan Swift, biting satire, and early novel forms.*
  * [`the-age-of-sensibility.html`](the-age-of-sensibility.html) (1745 – 1785/1798 A.D.) — *Age of Dr. Samuel Johnson, rise of dictionary, early Gothic fiction, and pre-Romantic poetry.*


### 5. 🌿 Romantic Period (1798 – 1832 A.D.)
* **Main Hub Page:** [`the-romantic-period.html`](the-romantic-period.html)
* *Golden Age of English Poetry — Wordsworth, Coleridge, Keats, Shelley, Byron, and Jane Austen.*


### 6. ⚙️ Victorian Period (1832 – 1901 A.D.)
* **Main Hub Page:** [`the-victorian-period.html`](the-victorian-period.html)
* **Sub-Movements:**
  * [`the-pre-raphaelites.html`](the-pre-raphaelites.html) (1848 – 1860 A.D.) — *Detailed sensuous art and poetry, Rossetti siblings, and medievalism.*
  * [`aestheticism-and-decadence.html`](aestheticism-and-decadence.html) (1880 – 1901 A.D.) — *"Art for Art's Sake", Oscar Wilde, Walter Pater, and sensory refinement.*


### 7. 🏙️ Modern Period (1901 – 1939 A.D.)
* **Main Hub Page:** [`the-modern-period.html`](the-modern-period.html)
* **Sub-Periods:**
  * [`the-edwardian-period.html`](the-edwardian-period.html) (1901 – 1910 A.D.) — *Social criticism and transition from Victorian rigidity.*
  * [`the-georgian-period.html`](the-georgian-period.html) (1910 – 1936 A.D.) — *Pastoral poetry preceding WWI and High Modernism.*


### 8. 🌐 Post-Modern & Contemporary Period (1939 – Present)
* **Main Hub Page:** [`the-post-modern-period.html`](the-post-modern-period.html) / [`the-contemporary-period.html`](the-contemporary-period.html)
* *Metafiction, Theatre of the Absurd, post-colonial voices, climate fiction (cli-fi), digital storytelling, and global identity.*
